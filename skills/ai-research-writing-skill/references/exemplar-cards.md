# Exemplar Cards

Use an exemplar card when the user supplies a strong paper as a writing
reference or asks to build a venue-matched writing library. Extract reusable
rhetorical structure, never task content or wording.

Record the source and venue, story arc, abstract sentence functions,
introduction paragraph roles, method presentation order, evidence organization,
citation patterns, reusable techniques, excluded anti-patterns, and a
do-not-copy boundary.

Exclude defensive framing, formula dumping without narrative purpose,
number-only abstracts, unsupported hype, and source-specific technical claims.
Store user-approved cards under `references/exemplars/cards/` and index them in
`references/exemplars/index.md`. Creating or registering a reusable card needs
user intent; reading an existing card does not need another confirmation.
